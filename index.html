Async Investments (Pty) Ltd is a Botswana-based business consultant specialising in company registration, company secretarial services and taxation — founded in 2010.

Our story · Est. 2010

Started in 2010 to close the gap between having a business idea and having a registered business.

Async Investments was founded in 2010 on a simple observation: most people who want to start a business in Botswana don't get stuck on the idea — they get stuck on the process. Which forms go to CIPA. What a company secretary is actually required to do under the Companies Act. When to register for tax with BURS. Whether their business needs a PPADB code at all.

We took the name "Async" from the way we work: your registration, your secretarial compliance and your tax filings run in parallel, handled by us, while you keep running the parts of the business only you can run. More than a decade later, that's still the job.

01

Mission

Make company registration, secretarial compliance and tax filing simple, fixed-fee and fast for every founder in Botswana — not just those who can afford a law firm.

02

What makes us different

Most agents specialise in one filing. We cover registration, secretarial and tax under one engagement, so nothing falls between two providers who don't talk to each other.

03

How we work

Plain-English guidance first, paperwork second. We tell you what's actually required before we file anything on your behalf.
